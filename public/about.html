About Fuldej Integrated Services Ltd

A multidisciplinary Nigerian business delivering innovative digital solutions, enterprise technology, and sustainable business development to drive long-term societal value.

Who We Are

Catalyzing Sustainable Growth Across Nigeria

Fuldej Integrated Services Ltd is an incorporated Nigerian enterprise registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC registration: RC 7360949). We operate at the intersection of technology, digital transformation, strategic consulting, and essential community infrastructure.

Our multidisciplinary model brings together engineers, digital technologists, business analysts, and development practitioners. We address key operational bottlenecks for private enterprises, government agencies, non-governmental organisations, and emerging grassroots entrepreneurs.

Core Purpose

Mission, Vision & Business Philosophy

The principles guiding our investments, technical decisions, and strategic partnerships.

Our Vision

To become a leading African enterprise recognized for delivering innovative, technology-driven, and sustainable solutions that create long-term value for businesses, public institutions, and communities.

Our Mission

To provide professional engineering, innovative digital technologies, strategic advisory, and impactful infrastructure projects that promote economic development, financial literacy, youth entrepreneurship, and sustainable growth.

What Defines Us

Our Core Values

01

Integrity & Transparency
