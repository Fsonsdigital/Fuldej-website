Legal & Data Governance

Corporate Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 21 October 2026 · Official Domain: https://fuldej.com.ng

1. Introduction

Fuldej Integrated Services Ltd ("Fuldej", "we", "us", or "our"), incorporated under the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria (CAC Registration: RC 7360949), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal or commercial information you share with us through our corporate website (https://fuldej.com.ng).

2. Information We Collect

Our website is structured to be informational and transparent. We only collect personal information that you voluntarily submit through our online contact form, partnership inquiries, or direct email communication:

  • Contact Identifiers: Name, professional title, and organization or company name.
  • Communication Details: Corporate email address and phone number (if provided).
  • Inquiry Content: Project specifications, service requests, or partnership scope details submitted in the message field.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

We use the information collected exclusively for legitimate corporate purposes, including:

  • Responding directly to your service requests, proposal inquiries, or partnership discussions.
  • Transmitting requested technical briefs, company profile documents, or commercial estimates.
  • Fulfilling our obligations under prospective or active service agreements.
  • Maintaining internal administrative correspondence and compliance records.

4. Cookies and Analytical Tools

This website does not deploy invasive commercial tracking cookies, third-party marketing trackers, or automated behavioral advertising pixels. Standard anonymous server access logs (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp) may be temporarily processed by our hosting infrastructure solely for security auditing, DDoS mitigation, and server health monitoring.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Fuldej Integrated Services Ltd does not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your contact information to third parties. Information is only accessed by authorized personnel and verified technical service providers (such as secure email hosts) necessary to process your inquiry, or when strictly required by Nigerian regulatory authorities under applicable law.

6. Data Retention & Security

We implement reasonable technical, operational, and physical security measures to safeguard submitted data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Inquiries that do not materialize into ongoing business relationships are periodically purged in accordance with our internal data retention guidelines.

7. Your Rights

As a visitor or prospective partner, you maintain the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold personal records regarding your communications.
  • Request correction or updating of any inaccurate contact details.
  • Request the deletion or removal of your contact information from our active communication records.
